# Midnight Harbour

**Stock**: One of the three numbered turns in a shift, independent of the number of balls currently on the table.
**Dock lock**: The left sink that holds a ball after all three lock buoys are marked.
**Kickout**: A sink that catches and ejects a ball back onto the playfield.
**Multiball**: Two simultaneously live balls released by making the second dock shot.
**Jackpot**: The once-per-multiball lighthouse award while its jackpot light is on.
**Rescue**: The original buoy → bumper charge → lighthouse objective, retained alongside the dock feature.
