# Midnight Harbour
An original pinball table about guiding a rescue vessel home.

## Language
**Buoy**: One of three navigation targets that mark the rescue route. _Avoid_: drop target.
**Beacon**: The central lighthouse target that completes a charged rescue. _Avoid_: jackpot hole.
**Rescue**: A sequence of marking three buoys, charging with five bumper hits, then striking the beacon. _Avoid_: level.
**Ball**: One of three attempts in a game, ending when the ball drains. _Avoid_: life.
**Bonus**: Points accumulated during a ball and awarded after its drain unless tilted.
**Tilt**: The penalty for three rapid nudges: flippers and scoring stop until drain.
